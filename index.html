Construction ERP in Singapore

Singapore contractors run the money on a local ERP and the site on a separate field app, and nothing connects the two. The opening is a SOPA-native “claims-to-cash” layer that turns site progress into payment claims and InvoiceNow e-invoices.

Prepared by Dinesh Bharathi · As of 13 October 2026 · Prices from public sources; most vendors quote privately, so treat figures as ranges.

Summary

70%of A1 contractors use Novade for permits to work
150+construction clients claimed by Synergix, the local ERP leader
Apr 2031all GST-registered firms must e-invoice via InvoiceNow
S$50kBCA Built Environment PSG cap for digital solutions (up to 50%)
  • Market structure: local customised ERPs (Synergix, HashMicro, Multiable) serve SMEs; global cloud ERPs (NetSuite, Dynamics 365, Acumatica, Odoo) serve mid-size firms but lack Singapore construction logic; SAP serves the largest groups; Novade and Procore run the site.
  • Prices: from about USD 3,000 a year in licences (Odoo, 15 users) to USD 15,000–80,000 a year (Procore). Local ERPs charge SGD 13,000–40,000+ per implementation.
  • Buying triggers: the InvoiceNow mandate and grants. Enterprise Singapore's PSG became EDGE on 30 Sep 2026; BCA's construction PSG runs until Dec 2026.
  • Biggest gaps: field-to-finance disconnect, no SOPA-native claims tool at SME price, subcontractors priced out, no claim-to-e-invoice automation, renewal price shocks after the grant year.
  • Recommended play: build the claims layer first, integrate with existing accounting, land via main contractors and onboard their subcontractors free, get grant-listed, then expand toward full ERP.

Vendors: pros, cons and price

Twelve products matter. Rankings published on Programming Insider, e27 and ERP Today place Multiable first and link to Multiable's site; HashMicro's blog ranks HashMicro first. Points from those sources are marked vendor-sourced.

Synergix

Local ERP · incumbent since 1990

150+ construction clients claimed, including Woh Hup, BHCC, Obayashi Singapore, Ley Choon and Poh Wah.

Pros
  • Deepest local construction workflows: cost-code budgets, subcon advance offset, auto back-charges
  • IRAS-registered accounting
  • Long-time grant pre-approved vendor
  • Strong support reputation
Cons
  • Priced per implementation; every client is a custom project
  • Learning curve and long rollouts
  • Only one public verified review
  • Weak field app; firms add a second tool
PriceSGD 13,000–40,000+ implementation, plus subscription

HashMicro

Local ERP · marketing-led challenger

Modular cloud ERP with tender/BOQ, budgets, procurement, site inventory, subcontractor billing and S-curves.

Pros
  • Broad construction feature list
  • ERP, HR, CRM and assets in one vendor
  • Easy to find and demo
Cons
  • Opaque per-feature pricing
  • 8–9 public reviews, all 5/5, mostly manufacturing
  • Few independent Singapore construction references
PriceNot published; one-time per module

Multiable aiM18

Regional ERP · Hong Kong

6,000+ Asian customers; IRAS Accounting Software Register listing with GST F5 and audit-file output.

Pros
  • IRAS ASR-registered
  • No-code workflow setup
  • Direct vendor support vendor-sourced
Cons
  • Extra charge for weekend support
  • Priced above firms under 10 staff
  • No named Singapore contractor case studies found
PriceNot published; quote

eBUILD & ProjectVIEW

Minor construction ERPs

Listed on Capterra Singapore. ProjectVIEW targets large contractors and marine/offshore yards; eBUILD targets developers and contractors.

Pros
  • Construction-specific from the start
  • Cloud delivery
Cons
  • Little public Singapore customer data
  • No public pricing
PriceNot published

Oracle NetSuite

Global cloud ERP

Chosen by mid-size and regional firms that need multi-entity consolidation.

Pros
  • Strong multi-subsidiary, multi-currency
  • Mature platform and partner base
  • Has had fixed-price grant packages
Cons
  • No out-of-box SOPA claims or retention
  • Renewal price increases; cost is top complaint
  • Construction needs add-ons
PricePast 5-user grant package: SGD 23,005 year one after discounts

Dynamics 365 Business Central

Global cloud ERP · Microsoft

The default for firms already on Microsoft 365.

Pros
  • Office, Teams and Copilot integration
  • Transparent per-user price; cheap light-user seats
  • Large Singapore partner base
Cons

  • List price rose Nov 2025 (USD 70→80)
  • Cannot mix Essentials and Premium users
PriceUSD 80 / 110 / 8 per user per month, plus partner

Acumatica Construction

Global cloud ERP · US

Dedicated construction edition with job costing and change orders.

Pros
  • Unlimited users, resource-based pricing
  • Real construction edition with mobile field access
  • Bundled fixed-price package
Cons
  • US billing logic, not SOPA
  • Weak on CPF and levy vendor-sourced
  • Small Singapore reseller network
PriceFrom under USD 1,500/month incl. implementation and support

Odoo

Open-source ERP

Popular with cost-conscious SMEs; Singapore gets a low regional price list.

Pros
  • Cheapest licence in the market
  • All apps included; mobile-friendly
  • Free Community edition
Cons
  • No native SOPA or retention; partners build it
  • Partner quality varies widely
  • Custom code makes upgrades painful
PriceUSD 16.90–25.50 per user per month (SG list), plus partner

SAP S/4HANA

Tier-1 ERP

For the largest contractors and government-linked groups.

Pros
  • Deepest controls and audit trails
  • Scales to mega-projects
Cons
  • Multi-year implementations
  • Very high total cost; specialist consultants
PriceEnterprise quote; not relevant to SMEs

Novade

Field platform · Singapore, 2014

The site leader: safety, quality, progress and permits to work.

Pros
  • 70% of A1 contractors for permits; 200K permits a month
  • Compliant ePTW with offline mode
  • Supplies PUB, DSTA, PA, BCA; SG data centre
  • Free for 5 users and 5 projects
Cons
  • Not an ERP: no accounting, billing or claims
  • Attendance and timesheets only on Enterprise
  • Clients skew to large contractors
PriceFree · Standard from USD 39/user/month · Premium & Enterprise on quote

Procore

Construction PM · US, ASEAN hub in SG

Best-in-class project management for large and multinational contractors.

Pros
  • RFIs, submittals, drawings, change orders
  • Unlimited users
  • Large integration ecosystem
Cons
  • Bill grows with your revenue
  • 10–14% renewal increases commonly reported
  • No SOPA or InvoiceNow localisation found
PriceUSD 15,000–30,000/yr small, 30,000–80,000 mid-size (third-party estimates)

Chillaccount & Payapps

Niche tools

Chillaccount is low-cost accounting for small subcontractors. Payapps is an Australian claims platform used by 34 of Australia's top 50 builders.

Pros
  • Chillaccount: cheap and simple
  • Payapps: proves a claims-only product can win a market
Cons
  • Chillaccount: limited past ~50 staff vendor-sourced
  • Payapps: no Singapore SOPA version found
PricePayapps from AUD 49 per contract per month

Five pricing models

  • Per implementation: Synergix, HashMicro, Multiable
  • Per user per month: Dynamics, Odoo, Novade
  • Base + users + setup: NetSuite
  • Unlimited users: Acumatica
  • % of construction volume: Procore (about 0.1–0.2% of hard costs)

Hidden costs

  • Renewal increases (Procore 10–14%; Microsoft about 14% in Nov 2025)
  • Partner fees for construction customisation
  • A second system for the field
  • Paid weekend support (Multiable)

What is missing

Eight gaps stand out. The first, second and fourth compound into the single biggest opportunity.

GAP 1

Field and finance are split

Novade has no billing; Synergix has no strong field app. Firms run both, joined by Excel.

Main contractors with 5–50 projects
GAP 2

No SOPA claims tool at SME price

Payapps does this in Australia; no Singapore version found. QS teams track claims in spreadsheets.

Subcontractors, QS teams
GAP 3

Subcontractors priced out

Synergix starts at SGD 13,000; Procore scales with revenue; Odoo needs a paid partner.

Trade and M&E firms under 50 staff
GAP 4

Claim → InvoiceNow is manual

Tax invoices follow certified amounts. No vendor shows an end-to-end claim-to-e-invoice flow.

Every GST-registered contractor to 2031
GAP 5

Foreign-workforce operations are thin

Novade attendance is Enterprise-only; lower plans offer English, French and Bahasa only.

Contractors with large foreign crews
GAP 6

Opaque pricing, long rollouts

Local ERPs quote per implementation and customise every install.

First-time ERP buyers
GAP 7

Renewal shock after the grant year

Grants pay year one; Procore and NetSuite renewals rise.

Every grant-funded buyer
GAP 8

AI is marketing, not workflow

Chatbots and form builders exist; nobody checks a subcon claim against measured progress and drafts the response.

QS and contracts teams

How we address the gaps

Don't build another full ERP first. Build the claims-to-cash layer — site progress → SOPA claim → certified amount → InvoiceNow invoice — that plugs into the accounting system a contractor already uses. Then grow into cost control and full ERP.

Go-to-market

  • Target: SME main contractors below A1, plus their trade and M&E subcontractors
  • Grant-list first: pre-approval under BCA's digital plan and EDGE
  • Land via the main contractor: onboard its 20–50 subcontractors free
  • Channels: QS consultancies, accountants, corporate-secretary firms
  • Lead with InvoiceNow: every IRAS date notice is a buying trigger
  • Proof: named case studies with measured outcomes

Risks and next steps

Risks

  • Incumbent response: Novade could add claims; Synergix could add a field app
  • Grant transition: BCA's PSG moves into EDGE in Dec 2026; rules may change
  • Small market: plan Malaysia and Indonesia from the start
  • Legal precision: SOPA logic needs a construction lawyer's review
  • Site adoption: offline mode and simple UI are mandatory
